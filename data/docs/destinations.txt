LADAKH
Ladakh, located in the northernmost part of India, is known for its stunning barren landscapes, Buddhist monasteries, and high-altitude passes. Wanderon's Ladakh trip typically spans 6-7 days and covers Leh, Nubra Valley, Pangong Lake, and Khardung La pass. The trip includes monastery visits, camel rides on sand dunes, and camping by Pangong Lake. Best season: June to September. Altitude ranges from 3,500m to 5,600m — acclimatization days are built into the itinerary.

SPITI VALLEY
Spiti Valley in Himachal Pradesh is one of the most remote and pristine valleys in India. The Wanderon Spiti Circuit covers Manali, Kunzum Pass, Kaza, Key Monastery, Chandratal Lake, and Chicham Bridge. The trip is 7-8 days long and involves long drives through dramatic mountain terrain. Best season: June to October. Roads are closed in winter due to heavy snowfall.

MEGHALAYA
Meghalaya, the "abode of clouds" in northeast India, offers lush green landscapes, living root bridges, crystal clear rivers, and unique Khasi culture. Wanderon's Meghalaya trip covers Shillong, Cherrapunji, Dawki, and Nongriat village. The trek to the famous double-decker living root bridge is a highlight. Duration: 5-6 days. Best season: October to May.

GOA
Wanderon's Goa experience goes beyond beaches. The trip includes exploring Old Goa's Portuguese heritage, spice plantations, Dudhsagar waterfall, local markets, and beach activities. Both North and South Goa are covered. Duration: 3-4 days. Best season: November to March, though monsoon trips (July-August) have their own charm.

KASHMIR
Kashmir trips with Wanderon cover Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and sometimes Sonmarg. Activities include shikara rides on Dal Lake, gondola rides in Gulmarg, and treks through alpine meadows. Duration: 5-6 days. Best season: March to October for summer trips, December to February for snow experiences.

MANALI & KASOL
The Manali-Kasol circuit is one of Wanderon's most popular beginner trips. It includes the Manali old town, Solang Valley, Jogini waterfall trek, Kasol village walks along Parvati River, and the Kheerganga trek. Duration: 4-5 days. Best season: Year-round, with different experiences each season — snow in winter, flowers in spring, green valleys in monsoon.

RISHIKESH
Rishikesh is the adventure capital of India. Wanderon's Rishikesh trip features white-water rafting on the Ganges, bungee jumping, cliff jumping, camping on the riverside, and evening Ganga Aarti. Duration: 2-3 days. Best season: September to June (rafting closes during monsoon).

VIETNAM
Wanderon's Vietnam trip covers Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh, and Hoi An over 7-8 days. Highlights include overnight cruises on Ha Long Bay, exploring the ancient town of Hoi An, and Vietnamese street food tours. This is one of Wanderon's most popular international trips.
