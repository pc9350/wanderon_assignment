PACKING SMART
For mountain trips, pack in layers — temperatures can swing 20+ degrees between day and night. A good base layer, fleece, and waterproof jacket are non-negotiable for places like Ladakh or Spiti. For beach destinations, light cotton clothes and swimwear are sufficient.

ALTITUDE SICKNESS
If your trip goes above 3,000m (Ladakh, Spiti), take altitude seriously. Stay hydrated, avoid alcohol for the first couple of days, and don't overexert yourself. Symptoms include headache, nausea, and breathlessness. Diamox can help but consult a doctor before the trip. Wanderon itineraries include acclimatization days for high-altitude destinations.

PHOTOGRAPHY TIPS
Mountain destinations like Ladakh and Spiti offer incredible photography opportunities. Golden hour (sunrise/sunset) is magical at altitude. For Meghalaya, carry a waterproof bag for your camera — it rains a lot. For stars in Spiti, a tripod and manual camera settings are essential. Phone cameras work great for most travelers these days.

BUDGETING
Beyond the trip cost, budget for: flights/trains to the departure city, personal shopping, optional adventure activities (paragliding, rafting, etc.), snacks and drinks, and tips for local guides. A rough estimate of Rs 3,000-5,000 extra per person covers most additional expenses on domestic trips.

SOLO TRAVEL
About 60% of Wanderon travelers are solo. If this is your first solo trip, a group trip is the perfect way to start. You'll be matched with a roommate of the same gender, and the group activities are designed to break the ice quickly. Most solo travelers leave with lifelong friendships.

RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL
Wanderon is committed to responsible tourism. Travelers are encouraged to carry reusable bottles, avoid single-use plastics, respect local customs and traditions, and minimize their environmental footprint. Some trips include community interaction and local homestays that directly benefit host communities.

CONNECTIVITY
Don't expect reliable internet in remote destinations like Spiti, Ladakh, or parts of Meghalaya. Use the opportunity to disconnect. If you need to stay reachable, inform your trip leader — they usually know which spots have network coverage. Downloading offline maps before the trip is always a good idea.

FOOD AND DIETARY NEEDS
Wanderon tries to accommodate dietary preferences when informed in advance. Vegetarian options are available on all trips. For specific requirements (vegan, gluten-free, allergies), mention them during booking so the team can plan accordingly. In remote areas, options may be limited.
