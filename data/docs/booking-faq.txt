BOOKING PROCESS
To book a Wanderon trip, visit the website and select your preferred destination and date. A booking advance of Rs 3,000 per person is required to confirm your spot. The remaining amount must be paid 10 days before the trip departure date.

GROUP SIZE
Each Wanderon trip batch has 10-15 travelers. This small group size ensures a more personal and immersive experience. If you're a solo traveler, don't worry — most Wanderon travelers are solo, and the trips are designed to help you connect with like-minded people.

CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY
If you cancel 15 or more days before the trip departure, you get a full refund. Between 7-14 days, a 50% refund is provided. No refunds are given for cancellations made less than 7 days before departure. Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
Standard trips include: accommodation, local transport during the trip, breakfast and dinner, a trip leader, and planned activities. Flights or trains to/from the departure city are NOT included — travelers need to arrange their own travel to the starting point.

WHAT TO PACK
Essentials include: comfortable trekking shoes, layered clothing (temperatures vary by destination), rain gear, sunscreen, personal medications, a good backpack (40-60L recommended for multi-day trips), power bank, and a reusable water bottle. Specific packing lists are sent after booking confirmation.

AGE REQUIREMENTS
Most Wanderon trips are designed for travelers aged 18-40. Some relaxed trips (like Goa or Kerala) accept travelers up to 45-50. Adventure-heavy trips like Ladakh or Spiti may have stricter age limits due to altitude and physical demands. Minors must be accompanied by a guardian.

TRAVEL INSURANCE
Travel insurance is recommended but not mandatory for domestic trips. For international trips (Vietnam, Thailand, Bali), travel insurance with medical coverage is strongly recommended. Wanderon can connect travelers with partner insurance providers.

SAFETY
All trips have an experienced trip leader trained in first aid. Emergency contacts and nearest medical facilities are identified for each destination. For high-altitude trips, oximeters and emergency oxygen are carried. Wanderon maintains a 24/7 emergency helpline during active trips.

CUSTOMIZATION
Wanderon primarily runs fixed-itinerary group trips. However, for groups of 8 or more, customized private trips can be arranged with modified itineraries, dates, and inclusions. Contact the Wanderon team for custom trip inquiries.
