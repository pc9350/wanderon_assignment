Wanderon is a community-driven travel company based in India that organizes group trips and adventure experiences for young travelers. Founded with the belief that travel should be accessible, social, and transformative, Wanderon has grown into one of India's most popular group travel brands.

Wanderon trips are designed for solo travelers, couples, and small friend groups who want to explore offbeat destinations without the hassle of planning logistics. Each trip includes accommodation, transportation, meals (depending on the package), and curated experiences led by experienced trip leaders.

The company operates trips to over 50 destinations across India and international locations including Vietnam, Thailand, Bali, Sri Lanka, and Nepal. Popular domestic destinations include Ladakh, Spiti Valley, Meghalaya, Kashmir, Manali, Kasol, Rishikesh, and Goa.

Wanderon trip leaders are carefully selected locals or experienced travelers who know the destination inside out. They handle all on-ground logistics, from check-ins to activity coordination, so travelers can focus entirely on the experience.

The Wanderon community includes over 100,000 travelers who have been on trips together. The company encourages genuine connections through small group sizes (typically 10-15 people per batch) and shared experiences like bonfires, treks, and cultural immersion activities.

Wanderon's mission is to make meaningful travel experiences accessible to everyone, breaking the notion that adventure travel is only for seasoned backpackers. Whether it's a first-time solo traveler or a group of friends looking for a hassle-free getaway, Wanderon handles the planning so travelers can focus on making memories.
